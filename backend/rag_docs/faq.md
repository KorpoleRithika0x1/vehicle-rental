# Veloce Rentals — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Veloce Rentals?
Veloce Rentals is a vehicle rental platform where customers can browse and book cars, SUVs, vans, trucks, and bikes for short-term use.

## Vehicle types available
- **Car** — sedans and compact cars for city travel
- **SUV** — family and adventure vehicles with higher seating capacity
- **Van** — group travel and cargo-friendly options
- **Truck** — utility vehicles for work or heavy loads
- **Bike** — motorcycles and scooters for quick urban trips

## How do I book a vehicle?
1. Browse available vehicles on the dashboard or ask the assistant to recommend one.
2. Choose pickup and return dates.
3. Confirm the booking. Your booking is confirmed immediately once dates are available.

## Booking rules
- Return date must be after pickup date.
- Minimum rental period is 1 day.
- You cannot have overlapping approved or active bookings for the same dates.
- A vehicle must have available stock for your selected date range.

## How do I cancel a booking?
- You can cancel **approved** bookings only.
- Pending, active, completed, or already cancelled bookings cannot be cancelled through the standard cancel flow.
- To cancel, provide your booking ID to the assistant or use the My Bookings page.

## Payment
Bookings may require payment depending on your account setup. Check your booking details in My Bookings for payment status.

## Cities
Vehicles are available across multiple cities including Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune. Filter by city when browsing.

## Fuel types
Vehicles may use petrol, diesel, electric, or hybrid fuel. Electric vehicles (EVs) are ideal for city driving with lower running costs.

## Support
For booking help, vehicle recommendations, or cancellation requests, use the Veloce Assistant on your customer dashboard.
